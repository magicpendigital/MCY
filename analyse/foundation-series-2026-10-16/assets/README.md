## Visual inspection and limits

- All three show one ordinary adult with a consistent approximate character identity, sage shirt, forest trousers, terracotta mat, and warm ivory background. Full bodies are in frame; no embedded text, arrows, panels, or logos.
- Standing concept: clear complete figure and mat; suitable as a calm series/card cover. The stance is an editorial concept, not a verified teaching reference.
- Tabletop concept: hands-and-knees side view is legible. The generator added peripheral plants/furniture despite the minimal no-extra-props prompt. Spine, joint placement, hand/foot anatomy, camera angle and any instructional suitability need teacher/art review before a real lesson.
- Rest concept: supine bent-knee scene and folded blanket are visible. The far arm is occluded and the legs overlap in perspective; it must not be used as the sole technical reference for limb placement or transitions.
- Character continuity is approximate rather than locked. Any future instructional sequence needs controlled source references and frame-by-frame review, including entry, adjustment, alternatives and exit.
- These images establish tone and composition only. They do not demonstrate a validated progression, accessibility suitability, injury prevention, therapeutic benefit, or clinical safety.
