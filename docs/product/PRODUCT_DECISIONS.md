# MCY product decisions (detail)

Durable Owner decisions moved out of `AGENTS.md` on 2026-11-13 to keep the always-loaded file small (hard gate 6). The text is unchanged; `AGENTS.md` keeps the summary and links here. **Read the matching section before touching that area.** A newer Owner decision replaces the older line here (edit in place, do not append history; dated status goes to `docs/status/`).

- **Check-in and Journey:** the body map and a text region list are equal paths; "no discomfort", "unsure", "skip" and unselected are different. Stored region codes are locale-neutral (`neck`, `upper_back`, `lower_back`, `glute_*`, and paired `shoulder`, `upper_arm`, `elbow`, `forearm_wrist`, `hand`, `knee`, `foot` with `_left/_right/_unspecified`); never rewrite saved observations. Illustration (feminine default, masculine, neutral) is a display choice, never inferred gender or personalization. Journey shows real snapshots only, keeps gaps and multiple records per day, and never computes improvement percentages. Guests skip the energy/time step (Owner decision 2026-11-08).
- **Free YouTube courses:** 38 unlisted Shorts from the Owner's channel in three course shells, embedded with the privacy-enhanced domain; never download, re-upload or mirror them. Decorative thumbnails come from `i.ytimg.com`; playback progress is counted on the device only. Instructor Huyền My: the public name comes from her activated account, never hard-coded; her invitation email stays out of the repo. Captions are "not recorded" until verified. Draft benefits/cautions text is labelled as awaiting expert review; rights, safety and instructional review remain release gates. Changing channel metadata is an external action needing confirmation at Save time.
- **Reading units:** 14 server-held units, published only through the Owner's per-unit switch (public snapshot bucket) and an internal-wording guard; six were live at last check. Units with text only or draft wording stay hidden until rewritten and re-approved by a qualified editor.
- **Content workspace (plan: [analyse/MCY_CONTENT_WORKSPACE_AND_AI_PLAN_2026-11-08.md](../../analyse/MCY_CONTENT_WORKSPACE_AND_AI_PLAN_2026-11-08.md); details: [docs/content-review/README.md](../content-review/README.md)):** one versioned store (video lesson, course group, reading unit, screen text) authored by a teacher or MCY; a version is immutable and its hash is the review version. The Owner types a declaration for every version, **including own content** (self-review allowed, audit-logged, switchable per kind). A teacher edits only own video lessons; the Owner reviews but does not edit them. AI may suggest, never approves. Owner content shows "Lựa chọn của biên tập · MCY biên soạn"; a teacher name shows only from an active profile; imported content has no byline. Order: content first, then AI. Applied to staging: phases 1, 2a, 2b, 3, 4. **Applied to staging 2026-11-09 (phases 3 and 4):** teacher tab "Bài video của tôi", Owner notes, the public snapshot `content-published/manifest.json` (rebuilt on every visibility, display or review change) and the learner app reading it with a bundled fallback (invalid or missing snapshot = today's catalogue and order). Video lessons carry author-chosen browse `areas` (shipped in batch 8; Edge v11 accepts them). **Phase 4 complete (code pushed, not in Play/phone yet):** four Khám phá screen texts (`screenTextKeys` in the workspace contract) can come from the snapshot, every other text stays locked "đã chốt"; Edge v12 deployed and migration `20261110175347_content_snapshot_screens.sql` applied to staging (2026-11-11); no screen text yet; `manifest.json` still unwritten. Web Preview is ahead of Play and the phone (aef1f47). Deliberately not built: a lesson "level" (no expert-approved scale) and hiding the AI-image disclosure (the "HÌNH AI" label always shows). No teacher profile is activated. First teacher: Huyền My.
- **AI:** real inference is staging-only and **off**. The learner assistant only selects from an expert- and rights-reviewed catalog (empty today) and returns session IDs; it needs the server gate (`MCY_AI_ENABLE_STAGING`, `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`, spend-control acknowledgment, exact staging URL), a pilot cohort, current `ai_processing` consent, rate/token limits and a kill switch. Send minimal current-turn inputs only; never names, emails, notes or history; never log prompts or outputs. Money limits are managed by the Owner on OpenRouter (no backend budget engine, never ask for amounts). No separate "health data" consent until wearables are connected. AI code (still off): one eligible session needs no model call; optional `suitableEnergy`/`intents` signals; the AI offer is hidden for reported/tired/uncomfortable/uncertain check-ins; `tests/practice-safety-suite.test.mjs` is the pre-enable gate; open P1/P2 items are in the plan file.
- **Assistant slot (Owner 2026-11-16; design: [plans/backlog/ai-assistant-central-design.md](../../plans/backlog/ai-assistant-central-design.md)):** a guest reaches a playing lesson in at most 3 taps and check-in is optional, after watching. The assistant ("trợ lý") has a central slot on Hôm nay in three layers: (1) rule-based suggestion labelled "Gợi ý" with no AI wording, (2) a model that only ranks reviewed ids, (3) closed-vocabulary conversation. Real AI stays behind every gate above and, since the Owner's 2026-11-18 decision, is for **paying members only** (the server must check the entitlement); guests and free accounts get layer 1 only. Safety content is never paywalled. Free-text input at layer 3 is allowed in principle; its safeguards in the design are proposals that need an R3 plan, an extended safety suite, expert-reviewed stop terms and the Owner's data-policy approval before it is enabled. The OpenRouter budget is decided only by the Owner: agents never propose amounts or set, change or raise spend limits and gates. Name and tone (Owner 2026-11-17): the assistant is called **Chút** ("Trợ lý Chút"); tone approved as proposed: "mình" and "bạn", at most two short sentences, invitations not commands, no exclamation marks or emoji, no outcome claims or percentages, no guilt, fixed templates (never model-written) for every pain, stop or safety sentence. Its label reads "chưa dùng AI" until a real model runs, then "Gợi ý bởi AI · chỉ chọn trong bài đã được duyệt". Trademark clearance of the name is not done. UI design (draft for review, not shipped): https://claude.ai/artifact/Uxw44YQsBvUdCFffPfPcHg.
- **Monetisation (Owner 2026-11-18):** paid courses are planned for the future and AI is only for paying customers. Not in 1.0 (payments stay out of scope there). Decided 2026-11-18: hybrid pricing (subscription plan plus single-course purchase); AI comes with the plan and optionally with a course (price setup has an "includes AI" option); the Owner films paid courses; the free tier stays 38 clips, Chút layer 1 and Hành trình; phase F starts after Closed testing (entry: ≥1 reviewed paid course, named expert reviewer, payment and protected-video decisions, Play Console check for Vietnam). Decided 2026-11-21 (as proposed): entitlement table is the single source of truth, with A (own Supabase) or B (managed service) chosen at the start of phase F after license-tester trials; Android first, web later; refund numbers 7 days / 30% / 48 hours / reply within 2 working days; support channel is the Owner's email; **selling legal entity: the Owner will supply it later (registering with Google), never fill it in**; the named expert reviewer is the Owner account, with a design to invite or assign other reviewers ([plans/backlog/reviewer-role-design.md](../../plans/backlog/reviewer-role-design.md)); defaults: AI included with a course works only in that course's context for the course access period, per-person quota set by the Owner, no AI trial yet, no live classes in paid courses, monthly subscription first. Names, prices, cycles and AI quota amounts are the Owner's; agents never invent them. Analysis and open questions: [plans/backlog/paid-tier-ux-2026-11-18.md](../../plans/backlog/paid-tier-ux-2026-11-18.md). Do not sell or advertise AI before it runs for real, never put safety content or the 3-tap free path behind payment, and paid video cannot live on unlisted YouTube.
